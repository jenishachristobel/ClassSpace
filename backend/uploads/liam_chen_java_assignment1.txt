## Introduction
This lab report covers the design of a concurrent producer-consumer pipeline utilizing `java.util.concurrent.BlockingQueue` and custom `ReentrantLock` primitives.

## Architecture
- 4 Producer threads generating cryptographic hash tasks.
- 8 Consumer threads processing with adaptive backoff.
- Atomic counters tracking throughput without mutex contention.
